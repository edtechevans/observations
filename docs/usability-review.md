# Classroom Observations: leader task review

Automated browser checks are in `tests/reflection-browser.py`. They test the application, not real users, and do not establish WCAG conformance. Complete this short review with representative leaders before a wider launch.

## Tasks without a walkthrough

1. Select Elementary, find a common growth facet, compare observations of the teachers who chose it, and describe the three coverage categories. Check whether the participant distinguishes a missing observation from lack of practice.
2. Find a teacher, read their full goal and a recorded response, switch the timeline between all observations and the chosen facet, open the original observation, then use Back. Check whether they retain their place and understand the timeline's school-year scope.
3. Select a 30-day period, explain the actual dates and the separate goal-import date, copy the view, and reopen it. Check whether the same facet, cohort and filters are preserved.
4. Repeat finding a goal using only the keyboard. Repeat the comparison on a phone and with browser zoom at 200%. Check focus visibility, text readability, touch controls and whether Escape dismisses a hover without losing focus.

Record completion, any wrong interpretation, help requested, lost position and suggested changes. Do not collect teacher narratives in test logs. Prioritize interpretation and blockers over cosmetic preferences.

## Data boundaries

All counts are evidence coverage, not goal-attainment scores. Goal statements and recorded responses are preserved verbatim. A missing imported goal is not a statement that the teacher has no goal; a missing response is not a statement that the teacher did not reflect. This remains the existing public deployment requested by the school.
